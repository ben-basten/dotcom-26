isolved needed a new marketing site to improve conversions, modernize the site design, allow their site to scale with their content needs. They needed a variety of page templates including standard marketing pages, customer testimonials, events, press releases, blog, and downloadable resources.

Their site was built on a headless CMS, to empower authors to quickly and easily make updates to the content. This approach also allowed tightly controlling author permissions; CMS administrators can create fine-grained user roles that specify what types of content authors are allowed to modify and publish to meet their governance needs.

Algolia search is integrated to index all content, site wide. Whenever content is published or un-published from the CMS, the Algolia search index is updated to match. Algolia is used for the full-site search in the main navigation, as well as curated search experiences like filtering through press releases, blog posts, etc.
